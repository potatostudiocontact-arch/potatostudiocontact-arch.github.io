Privacy Policy – Zelqon

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Zelqon ("the App"), developed by Potato Studio ("we", "us"), handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. The App has no user accounts of its own.

Your game progress (levels, coins, pets, garden, settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. This data is collected and processed by Google.

3. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are completed, when players stop playing, and which features are used) so we can improve it. It collects app usage events, device information, an app instance ID and approximate location. This information does not identify you personally and is processed by Google.

4. Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the App uses it to show leaderboards and achievements. Google shares your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and your scores with the App and, on leaderboards, with other players. You can manage this in the Google Play Games app.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases (for example "Remove ads" or coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the App can deliver it.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders on your device (for example about your daily streak). No data is sent to us for this. You can turn reminders off in the App settings or in your device settings.

7. Consent and your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice in the App (Settings → Ad privacy choices). You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. The only third parties processing data are Google (AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play and Google Play Games Services), as described above. More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

10. Data retention and deletion

Your game progress stays on your device until you use Settings → Reset progress in the App or uninstall the App. For data processed by Google, see Google's Privacy Policy.

11. Security

Data processed by Google services is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

13. Contact
